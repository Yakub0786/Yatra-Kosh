Terms of Service

Last updated September 2026. Plain language, on purpose — the terms that apply when you book a stay or a tour with us.

1. What this site is

Yatra Kosh is a travel agency that lists hotels and organises fixed-departure package tours across India. By booking a stay or submitting a tour enquiry, you agree to the terms on this page.

2. Bookings and enquiries

Confirming a hotel booking generates a reservation reference and a confirmation to your email. Payment is collected by the property at check-in, not by us. Submitting a package enquiry starts a conversation with one of our advisors — it is not itself a confirmed booking until dates and payment terms are agreed.

3. Pricing

Prices shown are per night for stays, and per person for package tours, exclusive of GST unless stated. Rates can change until a booking is confirmed. Promotional codes such as WELCOME10 and TRIP15 apply the stated discount at checkout, subject to their individual conditions and expiry.

4. Cancellation policy

Hotel bookings carry free cancellation up to 72 hours before check-in; inside that window one night is charged by the property. Package tours are fully refundable up to 21 days before departure, 50% up to 7 days, and non-refundable inside 7 days, since the vehicle and permits are booked on your behalf by then.

5. Content and illustrations

Photographs are used to represent the destination or neighbourhood and may not depict the exact room or property you book. Where a real photo of a location is unavailable, we show an illustrated representation instead.

6. Data collection

We use analytics to improve the site, with your consent requested on your first visit. See our Privacy Policy for details on what is and isn't collected.
