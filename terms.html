Terms of Service

Last updated September 2026. Plain language, on purpose — this is a coursework demo site, not a real booking platform.

1. What this site is

Yatra Kosh is a student project built for a Behavioural Data Analytics and UX course. The destinations described are real; the hotels, packages, advisors, and reviews are fictional. No booking made here is real, and no payment is ever collected.

2. Bookings and enquiries

"Confirming" a hotel booking or submitting a package enquiry on this site does not create a real reservation. Confirmation numbers, boarding-pass style tickets, and advisor replies are simulated for demonstration purposes only.

3. Pricing

Prices shown are illustrative and do not reflect real rates for any actual property or tour operator. Coupon codes on this site (WELCOME10, FIRST500, TRIP15, STAY300) are demo functionality and have no monetary value.

4. Cancellation policy (as simulated)

For demonstration purposes, hotel bookings shown here would carry free cancellation up to 72 hours before check-in, and package tours a full refund up to 21 days before departure. These terms exist to give the site realistic behaviour, not to bind any real transaction.

5. Content and illustrations

Destination illustrations are generated in the browser. Where real photographs appear, they are sourced from Wikipedia and depict the real destination, not any specific (fictional) property listed on this site.

6. Data collection

This site records browsing behaviour for coursework analysis, with consent requested on first visit. See our Privacy Policy for details on what is and isn't collected.

Questions about this project can be sent through the Contact page.
