Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. This page exists to be genuinely accurate, since the data behind it is the actual subject of the coursework.

What is collected

With consent (asked on your first visit), this site records: pages viewed, buttons and links clicked, scroll depth, search queries, form interactions (started, submitted, abandoned, errors), device type, browser, screen size, approximate location (city-level, from IP address), and timestamps.

What is never collected

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and anything else typed into a booking, enquiry, contact, or review form stay in your browser only. They are never sent to the analytics layer, regardless of whether you accept tracking.

Why it's collected

This site is a research instrument for a Behavioural Data Analytics and UX university project. The data is used to analyse navigation patterns, conversion funnels, and usability — not for advertising, and not sold or shared with any third party.

Where it's stored

By default, events stay in your browser's local storage on your own device. If the site operator has configured a remote collection endpoint, events are also sent there for aggregate analysis. No data is stored with personal identifiers.

Your choices

Decline tracking on the consent banner and nothing is recorded — every feature still works normally. You can also clear this site's data at any time through your browser's settings, which removes any locally stored events, saved bookings, and enquiries.

Third-party services

Destination photos are requested from Wikipedia's public API. Fonts are served from Google Fonts. Both may see a basic request (like any website visit) but receive no personal data from this site.

Contact

Questions about data handling can be sent through the Contact page.
