About

Travel across India, planned properly

Yatra Kosh plans fixed-departure package tours and lists hand-picked hotels across twenty destinations in India — with honest write-ups, no booking fees, and advisors who know the ground.

Who we are

We're a small team of travel planners who got tired of booking sites that read like brochures. Every destination on this site is somewhere we've actually spent time, every write-up is what we'd tell a friend, and the sorting is by rating or price — never by who pays us the biggest commission.

You can plan a trip two ways: book a hotel yourself from our catalogue, or hand us your dates and budget and let an advisor build the whole itinerary. Either way, there's no booking fee — you pay the property or ground operator's rate, and we earn our commission from them, not from you.

How we work

Our fixed-departure packages come with a private car and driver, a real day-by-day plan, and named inclusions instead of vague "sightseeing as per itinerary" lines. Each region has a dedicated advisor who has driven the route and knows when a fort is least crowded or which mountain pass is open.

Hotels are paid for at the property on arrival, so you're never charged before you've seen the room. Free cancellation applies up to 72 hours before check-in on stays, and up to 21 days before departure on packages.
